# Manual testing 2026-11-24: the user's feedback (raw, translated)

Run: `rm -rf sandbox/trial && npm run dev:sandbox -- trial` (section 1.1).

## UI feedback 1 (Scan, Settings, Review)

### Overall
1. The UI looks bad. The user does not understand what is happening on the screens: it must be simplified.

### Sidebar
2. Neat and extensible: good, keep it.

### Scan
3. The block with the ring is huge, and it is not clear what is where.
4. The ring uses shades of grey on a dark grey background: it looks bad.
5. The labels are not intuitive: e.g. "Swap and sleep image" means nothing to the user.
6. "Comes back by itself when needed. Removing it costs only a little time." is not understood at all.
7. Every label must be reviewed and made plain and intuitive for a person.
8. "fresher-trial-tool", "fresher-trial-cache": unclear what these names are (the trial sandbox's items).

### Cards
9. The card's settings menu (Ignore ...): make the hover of its items more expressive.

### Settings
10. A number input becomes a slider from the minimum to the maximum, with a number input next to it.

### Review
11. The list is not liked. "Safe to remove" reads as the heading of everything below it, and Low sits inside it
    although Low is a different level from Safe (the part "Safe to remove" holds the levels Safe and Low, and its
    name repeats the Safe level's zone name). Something else is needed.

## UI feedback 2 (direction)

The user added an outside UX write-up to `NO_COMMIT/` (they disagree with much of it).

12. Wanted: a more laconic, understandable interface; short, clear descriptions; a clear separation of sections.
13. For the first release, hide the level High entirely.
14. Settings is overloaded too. Consider settings with a global search that jumps to a setting, and a sidebar with
    the settings' structure (a tree of sections).

## UI feedback 3 (on the first mockups of plan 042, direction "summary and list")

15. Settings (own sidebar tree + search): good, more structured and cleaner.
16. Sliders look good: dragging is easier than typing numbers.
17. The category details screen is fine: a tick next to each item, and it is clear what the item is.
18. Review looks bad: it is not clear which items were reviewed; the items blend into each other.
19. Scan looks terrible: too much information that conflicts; the used-space bar is not understood at all; headings
    such as "Rebuilds by itself" tell the user nothing.

## UI feedback 4 (on the second mockups: plain list)

20. Worse than the current app: it looks poor, like a plain HTML document. A balance is needed between how much data
    the screen shows and how beautiful the visuals are.
21. A chart of the used space is a must.
22. A separator between the chart and the items is a must.
23. Maybe bring back the tiles (cards) of the current app.

## UI feedback 5 (on the third mockups: disk card with a ring, separator, tiles)

24. This looks good (Scan and Review of the third mockups; Settings, its search, the category details and the menu
    hover from the first round).

## Logo

25. Recolour the logo to the app's colours: variant A chosen (graphite leaf, iris swoosh). Use the swoosh's gradient on
    the buttons, as a decorative element at the bottom, and as the scan's progress line; put the logo small in the
    sidebar beside the name.
26. Remove the decorative swoosh at the bottom of Scan; the rest (the logo in the sidebar, the swoosh sheen on the
    buttons, the scan's progress along the swoosh) looks interesting.
