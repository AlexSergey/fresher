# 033/02/03 — The UI

**Status:** 🔨 in progress · **Version:** 0.1.0 · **Date:** 2026-11-22 · **Modules:** app/ui

The interface's path for the module: Scan's cards for its two categories, each category's Details with its findings
and left-out line, the review, a cleanup to the Trash and put back, the module's switch and its day setting in
Settings. **No new component** and no business logic in the UI: titles, reasons, sizes and danger levels are the
core's. Model: the temporary files module's path, which has the same shape (one decision per file, a day setting):
`app/ui/src/test/temp-scan.ts`, `SCRIPTS.temp` in `app/ui/e2e/core-mock.ts`, its scenarios in
`app/ui/e2e/flows.spec.ts` (search `openTemp`), `tempSettings` in `visual.spec.ts` and `axe.spec.ts`. The design:
[`../00-design.md`](../00-design.md), sections 6 to 9.

## Files

- `app/ui/src/test/logs-scan.ts` (new) — a scan as the core streams it: the module `logs` in the group `system` with
  its two categories, both Safe; app logs `old-tool.log` (reason "In your Logs folder, unchanged for 30 days.") and
  `writer.1.log` (reason "In Writer in your Logs folder, unchanged for 30 days."); crash reports
  `Writer-2026-10-01-101010.ips` (reason "A crash or diagnostic report, unchanged for 30 days."); one left-out count
  (`logs.left-out.recent`, 1 item, in `logs.app-logs`); the module's switch and its setting `logs.older-than-days`
  (number, min 7, max 90, unit days, value 7). Its texts read from `modules/logs/locales/en.json` and the core's
  locale file, exported as `LOGS_DICTIONARY`, shared by the unit tests and the e2e mock. Only states the core can
  produce together (an item is never both offered and left out; no finding has an app).
- `app/ui/e2e/core-mock.ts` — the scan as one of the mock's scripts, `logs`, with its cleanup (each item `moved`)
  and its settings view, as `temp` has them.
- `app/ui/e2e/flows.spec.ts` — the scenarios, negatives first:
  - negative: "the left-out line of the logs is not an item: nothing on it can be selected";
  - positive: "old logs and old crash reports show their cards with their sizes and item counts"; "the details of Old
    logs of your apps show the category, Safe and its texts"; "an old log's finding names its file, its folder and its
    age"; "selecting the logs makes the review name Safe"; "the old logs go from the review to the Trash, and the
    cleanup is put back from the Cleanups"; "Settings shows the logs switch and the days field, which cannot go below
    7".
- `app/ui/e2e/visual.spec.ts`, `app/ui/e2e/axe.spec.ts` — the Details of `logs.app-logs` (its findings and left-out
  line) in the Details table (`['logs', 'Old logs of your apps', '1 item left out (…)', 'category-details-logs.png']`);
  Settings with the logs module's days field in view (`settings-logs.png`).
- `app/ui/e2e/screenshots/` — the new images, in both browser projects, looked at before committing.
