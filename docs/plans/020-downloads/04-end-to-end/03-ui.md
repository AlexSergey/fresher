**Status:** ✔️ done · **Version:** 0.1.0 · **Date:** 2026-11-19 · **Modules:** app/ui

The interface's path for the module: the group **Your files** in the sidebar, the module's page with its five
categories and its left-out lines, its unavailable notice before Downloads is allowed, the review, the cleanup and put
back, the settings. No new component: the module has no choices, and the unavailable notice is plan 006's

"UI e2e".

## Files

- `app/ui/src/test/downloads-scan.ts` (new) — a scan as the core streams it: the module `downloads` in the group
  `files` with its five categories (an unfinished download and its placeholder Low, a Safari download Medium, a Firefox
  installer Low with `app` `org.mozilla.firefox`, an old macOS installer Low, a big file High), the left-out counts
  (`browser-open` for one item, `recent` for another, never an item both offered and left out), the setting
  `downloads.older-than-days` and the switch; and a second state of the same module `Unavailable { missing: [Folder
  (Downloads)] }` with Downloads `NotAsked`. Its texts read from `modules/downloads/locales/en.json`, shared by the unit
  tests and the e2e mock. Only states the core can produce together.
- `app/ui/e2e/core-mock.ts` — the downloads scan and its unavailable state as two of the mock's scans.
- `app/ui/e2e/flows.spec.ts` — the scenarios, negatives first:
  - negative: "the downloads module is unavailable until Downloads is allowed, and says how to allow it"; "the
    left-out lines of downloads cannot be selected";
  - positive: "downloads and installers show under Your files with their size"; "each downloads category shows its
    danger and its five texts"; "selecting the installers and the big files makes the review name High"; "an
    unfinished download goes to the Trash with its placeholder and comes back"; "Settings shows the switch and the days
    for downloads".
- `app/ui/e2e/visual.spec.ts`, `axe.spec.ts` — the module's page, and its unavailable state.
- `app/ui/e2e/screenshots/` — the new images, looked at before committing.

## Conclusions

- `downloads-scan.ts` holds seven findings in the five categories, the left-outs (`browser-open` on the unfinished
  downloads, `recent` on the big files), the settings, the meaning group of the Firefox installer, and the unavailable
  state with Downloads `NotAsked`. The core mock has three scripts: `downloads`, `downloadsUnfinished` (the cleanup of
  the unfinished download and its placeholder) and `downloadsUnavailable`.
- The confirm, result, Cleanups and put back run on the installers and the big file (design scenario 4); the unfinished
  download and its placeholder is an extra scenario with its own cleanup.
- The axe run found that a disabled list row (an unavailable category) was not exposed as disabled, so its muted text
  failed the contrast check; `ListRow` now carries `aria-disabled` when it is disabled.
- The banner was done by plan 02/01; this step did not touch it.
- Looked at: the module page (Your files group, Old macOS installers expanded, High on the big files, both left-out
  lines) and the unavailable page (every category "Not available", the Downloads notice with its action), in both
  engines.
