# 023/02/03 — The UI

**Status:** 🔨 in progress · **Version:** 0.1.0 · **Date:** 2026-11-21 · **Modules:** app/ui

The interface's path for the module: Scan's cards for its three categories and a category's Details with its findings
and left-out lines (plans 027 and 028 removed the module pages), the review, the cleanup and put back, the settings switch. No new component:
a finding's title and reason are the core's texts. The design: [`../00-design.md`](../00-design.md), section 7.

## Files

- `app/ui/src/test/local-ai-models-scan.ts` (new) — a scan as the core streams it: the module `local-ai-models` in the
  group `development` with its three categories (`meta/tiny` and `gpt2` Low, `o/words` Low, `pub/model-a` Medium),
  one left-out (`app-open` on an LM Studio model; never an item both offered and left out), the switch. Its texts read
  from `modules/local-ai-models/locales/en.json`, shared by the unit tests and the e2e mock. Only states the core can
  produce together.
- `app/ui/e2e/core-mock.ts` — the scan as two of the mock's scripts: `localAiModels`, and `localAiModelsLmStudioOpen`
  (with LM Studio open every LM Studio model is left out, so that category has a left-out line and no finding).
- `app/ui/e2e/flows.spec.ts` — the scenarios, negatives first:
  - negative: "a local AI model kept while LM Studio is open cannot be selected";
  - positive: "local AI models show their cards with their sizes"; "each local AI models category shows its
    danger and its five texts"; "a model's finding names the model, the date and the download size"; "selecting an
    LM Studio model makes the review name Medium"; "a Hugging Face model goes to the Trash and comes back";
    "Settings shows the switch for local AI models".
- `app/ui/e2e/visual.spec.ts`, `axe.spec.ts` — the Details of the Hugging Face models category (its left-out line).
- `app/ui/e2e/screenshots/` — the new images, looked at before committing.

## Conclusions

Built as written, with one change forced by plans 027 and 028: the module has no page, so the cases run on Scan's
cards and a category's Details. Two mock scripts: `localAiModels`, and `localAiModelsLmStudioOpen`, because with LM
Studio open every LM Studio model is left out, and an item is never both offered and left out. The scan's texts are read
from the module's own locale file. No new component. The final fix wave corrected a header comment that named a vitest use that does not exist. A left-out line's size stays
whole (allocated and reclaimable of the walked model): the core sums the sizes of what it left out and the UI shows
allocated, so a zero there made the line read "0 bytes".
