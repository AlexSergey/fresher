# 019/03/03 — The choices on the module page and the UI e2e

**Status:** ✅ approved · **Version:** 0.1.0 · **Date:** 2026-11-18 · **Modules:** app/ui

The module uses the generic module page; what is new is the page's choices (any module that declares some), from
`ModuleInfo.choices` of 01/04. The group Apps comes from 01/04. After 01/04 and 02/01 (the texts). Creates
`app/ui/src/screens/module/module-choices.tsx` and `app/ui/src/test/app-leftovers-scan.ts`.

## Files

- `app/ui/src/state/selection.ts` — a `SelectionAction` `{ type: 'choose'; categories: readonly CategoryId[]; within:
  readonly CategoryId[] }`: the selection's categories of `within` (the module's) and the findings picked one by one
  in them are dropped, then `categories` are selected whole; nothing outside `within` changes. Its tests.
- `app/ui/src/screens/module/module-choices.tsx` (new) — the module's choices above its categories, one button each
  with its label and its description beside it (`<module>.choice.<name>.label`, `.description` from the module's
  dictionary); nothing when the module has none; a choice whose categories have no finding is disabled. Small and
  presentational: it dispatches `choose`. Its tests (negatives first): no choices → nothing rendered; a choice with no
  finding → disabled; then a click selects exactly its categories.
- `app/ui/src/screens/module/module-screen.tsx` — renders `ModuleChoices` for the module.
- `app/ui/src/test/app-leftovers-scan.ts` (new) — a scan as the core streams it: the module `app-leftovers` in the
  group Apps with its two choices, findings of `com.example.Gone` in the three categories (Safe caches and logs,
  Medium settings, High support folder; `app` the id), the left-out counts (`running` for one id, `used-recently` for
  another, never an id both offered and left out), the setting and the switch, the map with the id's line (not
  installed); its texts read from `modules/app-leftovers/locales/en.json`, shared by the unit tests and the e2e mock.
  Only states the core can produce together.
- `app/ui/e2e/core-mock.ts` — the app-leftovers scan as one of the mock's scans.
- `app/ui/e2e/flows.spec.ts` — the scenarios of the design's section 6, "UI e2e", negatives first:
  - negative: "the left-out lines of removed apps cannot be selected"; "a choice selects nothing outside its module";
  - positive: "removed apps' leftovers show under Apps with their size"; "each removed-app category shows its danger
    and its five texts"; "Remove everything selects the three categories and the review names High"; "Keep settings
    selects only the caches and the review names Safe"; "the caches of a removed app go to the Trash and come back";
    "Settings shows the switch and the days for removed apps"; "a removed app's line in the map is named by its id".
- `app/ui/e2e/visual.spec.ts`, `axe.spec.ts` — the module's page with its choices and left-out lines.
- `app/ui/e2e/screenshots/` — the new images, looked at before committing.
